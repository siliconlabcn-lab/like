PLAYER MODEL PROVENANCE — 2026-11-08

The seven GLB files in this directory are locally authored stylized interpretations.
They are not official scans, licensed sports-game assets, or photoreal reconstructions.
Each has an individual source face, hair treatment, shirt identity, number and height.
The shared MPFB/MakeHuman human topology is distributed under CC0 1.0.
See ../LICENSE-MPFB-CC0.txt.

Tools: Blender 4.2.23 LTS, local MPFB / MakeHuman assets.
Source geometry: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
Animation: local authored keyframes, six actions on a shared 53-bone rig.
No paid model generation service was used.

Courtois, Mbappe, Haaland, Neymar, Salah, Bellingham face materials:
Each uses the corresponding existing AI-created portrait from the project game/assets
directory, mapped onto curved physical face and eye geometry in Blender.
Original raster files are unmodified. Their front-view appearance is not a complete
three-dimensional likeness reconstruction; side/back appearance is an interpretation.
